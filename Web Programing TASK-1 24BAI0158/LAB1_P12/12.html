Emergency Helpline Numbers in India

Medical Emergency

Description: Provides immediate medical assistance and ambulance services.

Helpline Number: 108

Email: help@108ambulance.in

Police Emergency

Description: For reporting crimes and seeking police assistance.

Helpline Number: 112

Email: policehelp@india.gov.in

Fire Emergency

Description: For fire accidents and rescue operations.

Helpline Number: 101

Email: firehelp@india.gov.in

Women Safety Helpline

Description: Provides assistance and protection for women in distress.

Helpline Number: 181

Email: womenhelp@india.gov.in

Child Safety Helpline

Description: Helps children facing abuse, neglect, or exploitation.

Helpline Number: 1098

Email: childhelp@india.gov.in

Disaster Management

Description: Assistance during natural and man-made disasters.

Helpline Number: 1078

Email: ndma@india.gov.in

Road Accident Emergency

Description: Emergency response for road accidents.

Helpline Number: 1073

Email: roadhelp@india.gov.in

Senior Citizen Helpline

Description: Support and assistance for senior citizens.

Helpline Number: 14567

Email: seniorhelp@india.gov.in

Cyber Crime Helpline

Description: Reporting online fraud and cyber crimes.

Helpline Number: 1930

Email: cybercrime@india.gov.in

Poison Control

Description: Emergency assistance for poisoning cases.

Helpline Number: 1066

Email: poisonhelp@india.gov.in
